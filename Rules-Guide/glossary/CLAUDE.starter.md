# glossary starter rules — paste into YOUR workspace CLAUDE.md

## Controlled QA vocabulary — glossary
- **Home:** `QA-SetupKit/Rules-Guide/glossary/GLOSSARY.md` — every load-bearing term defined
  once, with its OWNER kit and schema home. On meaning conflicts between kit docs,
  the owner kit wins and the glossary entry gets fixed.
- **New load-bearing term → glossary entry in the same change**; a term used by ≥ 2
  kits without an entry is a defect. Renames = breaking changes (update owner +
  glossary + all references in one change, or don't).

Full rules: `QA-SetupKit/Rules-Guide/glossary/README.md` (§ Rules).
